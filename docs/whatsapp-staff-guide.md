# WhatsApp + dashboard — staff guide

The shop's WhatsApp number is connected to the AquaGear dashboard. **Keep using
WhatsApp Business on the phone exactly as before** — chats, replies, photos,
voice notes and calls all work the same.

## What you'll notice

- Every customer chat also appears in **Admin → Inbox**. Replies you send from
  the phone show there as **Staff (phone)**; replies sent from the dashboard show
  as **Staff (dashboard: name)** and also appear on the phone.
- When a customer asks to buy something, the dashboard prepares a **draft
  order**. Nothing is confirmed and no stock is taken until someone presses
  **Confirm** in the dashboard. Keep confirming details with the customer in the
  chat as usual.
- Turned off for 1:1 chats since the connection: disappearing messages, view
  once, live location. Broadcast lists are read-only.
- Group chats work as before but don't appear in the dashboard.

## Please

- **Open WhatsApp Business on the main phone at least once a week.** If it isn't
  opened for about 14 days, the dashboard disconnects.
- **Don't reinstall WhatsApp, move it to another phone, or change the number**
  without telling the admin first — any of those disconnects the dashboard.
- **Don't use WhatsApp for Windows** (or a WearOS watch) for customer replies —
  messages sent from them don't reach the dashboard. The Mac app and WhatsApp
  Web in a browser are fine.

## Replying from the dashboard

The dashboard can send free text only within **24 hours of the customer's last
message** (a Meta rule). After that it can only send pre-approved templates.
**The phone has no such limit** — you can always reply from the phone.

## If the dashboard says "Disconnected"

Tell the admin. They reconnect from **Admin → Meta → Connect WhatsApp Business
app**, scanning a QR code with the WhatsApp Business app on the phone. The phone
keeps working the whole time.
